# Structure -> Startup systems

__This chapter explains what systems are, what they are for and what kind of systems there are.__

So what is a system? A system is a function. It is as simple as that. There are two different kinds of systems:
- startup systems
- other systems, usually called something like "ECS systems"

__Startup systems__ get executed once. They exist to handle events.

__ECS systems__ get executed every frame or every certain amount of time. They exist to read and manipulate data.
